Privacy Policy

Happa Community App

We collect your name, phone number, and PIN to create and secure your account. Your shopping and family activity data is stored securely on Supabase servers.

What we collect: Name, phone number (hashed PIN), order history, family group membership, points earned, and app activity.

How we use it: To provide the Happa service, connect you with local shops, manage your family group, and improve the app.

Data sharing: We do not sell your data. Merchant partners see only orders placed with them.

Your rights: You may delete your account at any time. Deletion removes all personal data within 30 days.

Contact: For data requests, email support@happa.co.ke
